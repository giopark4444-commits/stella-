STELLA FUGAZ · "THE NODE OF ERDIA"

Screenplay — Film 1 (v2) · Production draft

English translation of GUION_P1_v2.md. Screenplay format, written from the canon in
PELI1_v2_material_base.md and TRATAMIENTO_narrado_P1.md.

🔒 Golden rule: Vorthan's shapeshifting is a secret until the climax. In this script, Vorthan is
written as a tyrant king, S1G1 as a robot, Marek as a loyal ally, and Selka as Vera's killer.
It is written as the audience lives it, not as we know it to be.

Conventions: sluglines INT./EXT. LOCATION — TIME. Action in present tense. CHARACTER + dialogue.
(parenthetical). Time cards as [ERDIA · YEAR X].

Canon adjustments (v2.1):
• Eyes: Vera and Selka have grey-blue eyes (visual consistency).
• Scar: Selka has a scar over her left eye from her cadet/young-soldier years onward (not as a baby or a child).
• Geography: Naio's laboratory is INSIDE the imperial palace (Naio is the King's scientist). That is why the flight lab → corridors → Orb Chamber is continuous, within one building.
• White hair: it is the family's naieli vow (Stella, Vera, Naio, Selka). The resistance and the pilots are NOT all white-haired. The allies who save Stella are 3 pilots of varied appearance.
• Weapons: energy or hand-to-hand only — never firearms (no guns). Violence is shown through light, not blood.
• Stella's wardrobe: civilian clothes (park) → "04" armor with the braid (lab/escape) → armor with short hair after she cuts the braid off.

Translation glossary: the invented terms are kept as in the English reference prompts —
luzagua (water-and-light), naieli / naiel (the people), nai (the sacred), the Orb, the light-key, Gix.

ACT ONE · 起 (KI) — ERDIA

SEQ. 1 — EXT. SURFACE OF ERDIA — DAY0:00 → 1:306 blocks · #1-6

Black.

A line of light splits the darkness: the rim of a planet coming into dawn. The camera falls toward it, passes through golden clouds, drops over living mountain ranges, over forests that breathe.

And it finds a crack. A dry fracture in the crust, like a scar nobody healed.

We descend into it. The light falls away behind us. The rock walls go from gold to grey, from grey to black. Deeper. Deeper. All the way to the very center of the world — where the heart of an entire planet ought to be beating.

Nothing beats.

It is hollow. Dead. A black, bottomless cavern where there was once life.

Total silence. Hold.

TITLE, over black: STELLA FUGAZ

SEQ. 2 — EXT. LUZAGUA PARK, THE GOLDEN CITY — DAY1:30 → 3:357 blocks · #7-13

HARD CUT to a sky that is blue, wide open, dazzling — and a burst of laughter that shatters the silence before it like a dropped glass.

STELLA (14) runs across the grass of a park. White hair streaming, barefoot, unstoppable. Beside her runs a river of luzagua: water and light at once, which people let slip through their hands without ever keeping any.

Stella touches a wristband on her arm. Out of the air ignites GIX — a small creature of light, half spark, half mischief — who slips away, flying. Stella chases him, laughing, dodging through the crowd.

Behind her, in no hurry, walks VERA (mother, ~40, white hair pinned up, grey-blue eyes).

VERA
Slow down. You'll crack your head open the day before your final trial.
STELLA
(not slowing, over her shoulder)
Mama, I'm the best in my class. I'm going to make you proud.

Stella stops dead. Something enormous has blocked out her sun.

It is a colossal MECHA. An ancient war machine, from back when Erdia conquered worlds — now kneeling forever, covered in flowers and creeping vines. A sleeping giant beneath a garden.

Stella looks up at it, tiny.

STELLA (CONT'D)
Why did we stop? Conquering planets. We were the strongest thing in the sky.

Vera catches up to her. This one she means. She crouches down to Stella's height.

VERA
That isn't as romantic as it sounds. We wanted all of it… and we nearly ended up with nothing.
(she gestures out at the river)
A person is like a river, Stella. Dam it up for yourself, and it rots.
(beat)
And it poisons everyone downstream.

Stella nods, very serious… and her eyes are already back on Gix, who's calling to her from a tree. She's fourteen. She doesn't understand it yet.

Vera notices. She smiles, a little sadly. She lets her go.

VERA (CONT'D)
I'm going to get your sister. See you at home for dinner.
STELLA
(already running)
Tell Selka I beat Gix this time!

Vera watches her go, laughing, white against the green.

The camera stays on Vera watching her daughter. A mother looking at her child on the last good day of her entire life — and only we know it.

SEQ. 3 — INT. NAIO'S LABORATORY — IMPERIAL PALACE — DAY3:35 → 6:4711 blocks · #14-24

The door opens and Stella comes in still laughing, Gix looping around her.

STELLA
You sent for me, what's so important that it can't wa—

Her laugh dies on its own.

Because she sees VERA and her father NAIO (~45, the King's scientist — his laboratory is inside the palace itself) standing in front of a data projection. Neither of them is laughing. They have the faces of people who have just seen something that cannot be unseen.

Behind them, in a floating bassinet, sleeps BABY SELKA (1 year old, grey-blue eyes).

STELLA (CONT'D)
(the smile falls away)
…Is everything okay?

Nobody answers her. Naio holds out a folded suit.

NAIO
Put it on. Quickly.

Stella obeys without understanding. She starts to dress. Naio comes closer, lowers his voice — like a man confessing something forbidden inside his own house.

NAIO (CONT'D)
Listen to me carefully. The King is not protecting us.
(beat)
He is eating the planet from the inside. And it's dying.

Behind him, the projection confirms it: the same descent from SEQ. 1, all the way down to that black, hollow heart. Erdia, emptying out from within.

STELLA
…That's not possible. The Orb gives life to everything. You taught me that.
NAIO
That's why he wants it.

Naio activates a machine over Stella's open hand. She expects him to put something into it — a jewel, an object. He puts nothing in. He prepares her. A light-key is etched beneath her skin, into her palm, with a brief, clean pain.

Stella grits her teeth.

NAIO (CONT'D)
The Orb is our blood, Stella. Only our family can touch it.
(he closes her hand)
What I just did to you lets you integrate it. Take it inside you. And once it's in there…
(he looks her in the eyes)
…only you could ever use it. No one else.

Stella doesn't get the chance to process it. Behind her, baby Selka wakes and starts to cry.

And Stella, on pure older-sister instinct, drops everything and goes to her.

She leans over the bassinet. She cups her hand. A spark of Gix drifts down and dances in her palm, like a firefly. She presses her forehead to the baby's.

And the two of them smile.

Selka settles instantly. A tiny, warm moment between sisters. (Hold onto this. It comes back.)

Footsteps. Many. Coming up the corridor.

Naio and Vera exchange a look. Time is up.

NAIO (CONT'D)
(fast, firm)
They're here. Take Selka. Go for the Orb.
(to Stella, taking her face in both hands)
When you have it, put it in your hand. And whatever happens… never, never let him have it. Do you hear me?
(beat)
I'll hold them.
STELLA
Papa, no—
NAIO
I love you. All three of you. Go.

The three of them embrace, far too briefly. Vera lifts the baby.

The main door opens.

In walks VORTHAN — the King. Tall, serene, with the smile of an old family friend. Robot soldiers behind him, filling the doorway like a shadow.

VORTHAN
(almost fondly)
Naio. Old friend.
(he takes in the scene, the suit, the empty bassinet)
You knew this day was coming.

Vera and Stella are already sliding toward the rear exit, slowly, not running yet. But the King's eyes see everything.

VORTHAN (CONT'D)
(without raising his voice)
Don't let them out.

Naio steps into their path. He activates a force field between the King and his family — it buys them three seconds.

The King looks at him almost with pity. And drops him with a single blow, like a man brushing a branch aside.

STELLA
(a choked scream)
PAPA!

Vera tears her away. They run.

SEQ. 4 — INT. PALACE CORRIDORS — CONTINUOUS6:47 → 7:373 blocks · #25-27

Vera and Stella run flat out. Vera holds baby Selka clamped against her chest. Behind them, far off but closing, the hum of the imperial robots.

Vera speaks in bursts, without breaking stride. And what she says is brutal in how clear it is.

VERA
Remember this, Stella: nothing matters more than that Orb. Nothing.
(she looks at her for a second)
Not me. Not your sister. Without it, the whole planet dies. Everyone. Do you understand?
STELLA
(running, eyes brimming)
I understand. You just take care of Selka.

Vera glances sideways at her. And in the middle of the escape, she smiles at her. A mother's smile, impossible in that moment, and stronger for it.

VERA
Whatever happens, get to the ship. I left it outside, at the exit that opens onto the park.

A wall bursts inward. The first robots come through.

Vera and Stella fight as they advance — Vera with one arm shielding the baby, Stella moving with the agility of someone who trained her whole life for a trial that was not this one. They cut a path through.

At the far end: the doors of the ORB CHAMBER.

SEQ. 5 — INT. ORB CHAMBER — CONTINUOUS7:37 → 10:068 blocks · #28-35

An immense chamber. At its center, suspended, the ORB — a sphere of pure luzagua, beating like the world's second heart.

Vera and Stella come in from one side. From the other, at the same moment, comes VORTHAN.

They look at each other across the chamber. A race decided in a single heartbeat.

VERA
Go for the Orb! NOW!

Stella breaks toward the sphere. But the robots close around Vera, who fights on protecting Selka, unable to follow her.

And then another one comes in.

S1G1. A different robot — dark, leaner, faster. Without the noise of the others. An assassin.

Vera sees it. And understands that this is no longer a fight. It's an execution.

She tucks the baby into the recess of a panel, far off, safe. And plants herself in front of S1G1.

She fights for real. She gives everything she has. It isn't enough.

S1G1 overpowers her. Vera goes down — and even falling, her last movement is to cover Selka with her body.

VERA (CONT'D)
(on the floor, screaming)
STELLA, RUN! DON'T LOOK BACK!

Stella is three steps from the Orb. Her hand outstretched.

And she does not obey.

She turns and runs to her mother with everything she has. She breaks the one rule. Out of love.

VERA (CONT'D)
NO! STAY BACK!

Too late. VORTHAN intercepts her. He catches her by the braid and lifts her off the floor, leaving her hanging, pinned, kicking at the air.

Stella weeps with rage and helplessness. The King, holding her by the hair, laughs under his breath.

VORTHAN
(soft, almost paternal)
So like your mother. Running toward what she loves.
(beat)
That's why you always lose.

On the floor, Vera gathers the last of what's left in her. She raises both hands toward her daughter—

—and forms a shield of light around Stella.

At the exact instant that S1G1, behind her, runs her through the heart.

STELLA
(a scream that breaks apart)
MAMA!

Vera looks at her. She holds the shield with the life running out of her. And her lips move one last time:

VERA
(almost no voice)
Run.

Stella, hanging by her hair, trapped, watches her mother die a meter away from her.

And she does the only thing she can do. She pulls a laser knife from her suit. She brings it to the back of her neck. And cuts off her braid in one stroke.

She drops free to the floor.

Vera's shield goes out with her.

SEQ. 6 — INT. ORB CHAMBER — CONTINUOUS10:06 → 11:164 blocks · #36-39

Stella rolls, gets up, and now she does run for the Orb — broken inside, eyes flooded, but running. She dodges one robot. Another. She jumps.

And she takes the Orb.

In the exact second her fingers touch it, the key in her palm ignites. The sphere integrates, shrinks, beats inside her hand. The wrist monitor reads: "MAX."

The suit's thrusters roar. Stella launches for a narrow duct high in the ceiling — the only way out.

Before she enters it, for a fraction of a second, she looks down.

She sees her mother, sprawled, still. She sees baby Selka crying beside the body. She sees them for the last time.

She grits her teeth. She enters the duct.

S1G1 fires its thrusters and chases her into the tunnel, gaining, centimeters from her boots—

—and jams in the opening. It thrashes. Sparks. EXPLODES.

The blast spits Stella out the other side, into open sky.

BELOW, in the chamber: Vera dead on the floor. The baby crying beside her. VORTHAN approaches, unhurried, and lifts Selka into his arms. The baby goes quiet, looking up at him.

Two robots bend to collect Vera's body. The King stops them with a gesture.

VORTHAN
(cold, not looking at her)
What are you doing? She's already dead.
(he turns away with the baby)
Get out there and find Stella. We need that Orb.

The robots drop the body. They go.

The King walks out with the baby in his arms, leaving Vera lying on the floor of her own home.

SEQ. 7 — EXT. SKY OVER THE GOLDEN CITY — CONTINUOUS11:16 → 12:345 blocks · #40-44

Stella is flung clear of the palace and lands on the ship Vera hid. She climbs in. Hands shaking. She fires it up.

She's barely off the ground when an IMPERIAL FLEET appears on the golden horizon. Dozens of ships.

The chase begins. And here we learn something: Stella flies like no one else. She threads the towers, scrapes the bridges, sends her pursuers slamming into buildings. She's brilliant. But there are too many.

An imperial ship frames her. The shot is about to fire—

—and it bursts, blown open from the side.

OTHER SHIPS appear. Allies. People who knew. They cut in, open fire, carve her a corridor through the sky.

VOICE (RADIO)
(a woman, with the calm of someone who has already decided to die)
Stella. Your mother told us what was happening.
(another ship sacrifices itself in the background)
We're here to protect you. Don't let our sacrifice be for nothing.
STELLA
(on the edge of tears)
Who are you? I don't know you! I don't—!
VOICE (RADIO)
Don't let anyone get that Orb. Anyone.
(beat)
Go far. Get strong. And come back one day and save us.
(beat)
We'll be waiting for you.

One by one, the allied ships put themselves in the way and blow apart, opening her a road to open space. Most of them die for her. But three are brought down without exploding — forced down, not destroyed — and the imperial fleet takes their three pilots alive. Stella sees all of it in her rear display: the ones who die, and the three they take prisoner.

And she's out. Into the void. Alone.

SEQ. 8 — INT./EXT. STELLA'S SHIP — DEEP SPACE — CONTINUOUS12:34 → 14:206 blocks · #45-50

The silence of space. Erdia, golden and sick, shrinking behind her.

A soft alarm. Then another. OXYGEN: CRITICAL.

Stella breathes slower and slower. She cries without a sound. She is fourteen years old, she has just watched her mother and a crowd of strangers die, and she is running out of air in the middle of nowhere.

She looks at her hand. The light-key. The Orb beating inside it.

And in an act of pure desperation — not knowing what she's doing — she puts her palm on the console.

The Orb's energy starts to flow into the ship. The panels light up one after another. The charge climbs. Climbs. The whole system starts to shake, to overload, to collapse—

—and the ship makes a HYPER-JUMP.

A burst of white light swallows the screen. The ship is torn out of its universe.

Total white.

SEQ. 9 — INT. PALACE, ROYAL BALCONY — ERDIA — [YEAR 0] — DAY14:20 → 15:425 blocks · #51-55

Under a sky that is still blue, VORTHAN addresses an immense crowd. A voice in mourning. Flawless.

VORTHAN
Today, we have been robbed.
(he lets the word land)
The Orb — the heart of Erdia, the source of everything we are — has been torn out of our world.

A murmur of horror across the plaza.

VORTHAN (CONT'D)
Without it, the light will go. The water will go. And our planet… will begin to die.

Behind him, displayed in cages, the THREE PILOTS who saved Stella — captive, beaten. (The rest of the allies died opening her the sky.)

VORTHAN (CONT'D)
And who has condemned us to this? One of our own.
(beat)
A child we gave everything to. Who chose to keep everyone's light for herself rather than let it run.
(the final word, like a sentence passed)
Stella. Thief. Traitor.

The crowd, who that morning would have loved her, begins to chant her name with hatred.

VORTHAN holds baby Selka in his arms, in front of them all. The baby looks out at the raging plaza, understanding none of it.

And very slowly, almost imperceptibly, the King strokes her head — like a father.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF ACT ONE.

ACT TWO · 承 (SHŌ) — THE EXILE / ERDIA ROTS

(Intercut montage: Stella's life in the other universe ↔ Erdia dying. Year cards on every jump. Because of the 1:4 dilation, Stella ages a little while her world falls apart a lot.)

SEQ. 10 — INT. THERON'S MOTHERSHIP — CARGO BAY — ?15:42 → 17:176 blocks · #56-61

Metallic dripping. Cold light.

Stella wakes with a jolt on the floor of a huge, dark bay. Her mother's ship, what's left of it, is clamped in mechanical arms like a hunted animal.

Stella sits up. The first thing she does — before anything else — is look at her hand. The light-key is still there. The Orb is still inside. She breathes.

An enormous shadow shifts in the gloom. THERON — the Hunter. Tall, of another species, with eyes that don't blink the way ours do. He has been wearing that face for centuries.

STELLA
(on guard, voice wrecked)
Where am I? Who are you? Did you… did you save me?
THERON
(not coming closer)
I didn't save you. I picked you up.
(beat)
There's a difference.

He approaches, slowly. He looks at Stella's hand. The glow of the Orb beneath the skin.

THERON (CONT'D)
That thing you're carrying shines. It shines like nothing I've seen in a long, long time.
(he looks her in the eyes)
And anything that shines like that… can be followed.

Stella closes her fist, hiding the hand. She doesn't understand yet that to him she isn't a lost child.

She's a trail.

SEQ. 11 — INT. MOTHERSHIP — CORRIDORS AND WORKSHOP — LATER17:17 → 19:248 blocks · #62-69

Theron's crew look at Stella the way you look at something that shouldn't be aboard. They shove her. They ignore her. They drop a plate of food on the floor at her feet.

VOSK (thug, big, scarred) blocks her path. Sizes her up.

VOSK
The boss's pet. The bait that breathes.
(he pushes her with one finger)
You know what happens to bait once the fish bites?

Stella knocks his hand away. Vosk grins — that's what he wanted. He throws the first punch.

They fight. Stella is fast, trained, but small; Vosk is a wall. He has her against the bulkhead when—

A stone hand the size of an anvil lands on Vosk's shoulder and moves him aside without effort.

BROG. A giant of rock, slow, silent, the last of his species. He says nothing. He just stands there, between Vosk and the girl.

Vosk spits and walks off.

From a workbench covered in parts, without looking up, NIMA speaks — a mechanic with four arms, all four busy at once.

NIMA
Don't mind Vosk. He barks so he doesn't have to think.
(now she does look at her)
There are two ways to break, kid. The kind that cries… and the kind that fixes things at three in the morning.
(back to her work)
Guess which one I am.

Stella sits down on the floor, exhausted, holding her injured hand. Brog sits beside her, enormous, in silence. He doesn't comfort her. He just stays.

THERON comes in.

STELLA
I want to go back to my planet. My family is there. My father, my sister—
THERON
Your planet is not your most urgent problem.
(beat)
There's something you need to understand, and you're not going to like it. Here, time doesn't run the way it does in your world.
(he looks at her)
One year of yours… is four of theirs.

Stella lifts her face, slowly taking in the size of that.

STELLA
(barely a voice)
…Four.
THERON
Every day you waste here, you lose four there. So don't waste them.

He leaves. Stella sits staring at her own hand, and at the weight of a clock running four times faster on the other side of the sky.

SEQ. 12 — EXT. ERDIA — RESISTANCE SHELTER — [ERDIA · YEAR 8] — NIGHT19:24 → 20:354 blocks · #70-73

TITLE CARD: ERDIA · YEAR 8.

Erdia isn't golden anymore. Fog. Scaffolding. The luzagua rivers run thinner, cloudier. In the distance, mine stacks cough smoke where there used to be gardens.

In a hidden shelter, a group of gaunt naieli gather around a leader with a warm, steady voice: MAREK. The kind of man people follow because he gives them their hope back.

MAREK
The King says we're like this because of Stella. Because of a thief.
(he looks around at his people)
But I ask you this: did a fourteen-year-old girl empty out an entire planet? Or did somebody need us hating someone, so we wouldn't look at him?

A murmur. They nod. Marek has given them a different enemy.

Beside him, silent, stands a HOODED FIGURE. We don't see the face. Only the hands — a woman's hands, strong — and a lock of white hair escaping the hood.

The figure watches Marek and the people. And lowers her head.

We don't know who she is. Not yet.

SEQ. 13 — EXT. SCRAP PLANET — FLEET OPERATION — [STELLA: ~YEAR 2]20:35 → 21:464 blocks · #74-77

An operation by Theron's fleet. Stella, a little older now, harder, moves through the debris with a group. Brog covers. Vosk takes point.

Something goes wrong. Local bounty hunters ambush them. In the chaos, Stella hides in a crevice, out of the enemy's line of sight. Safe.

And she sees VOSK looking at her from a distance. Their eyes meet.

Vosk picks up a rock. And throws it against the metal right beside her — a hard, flat clang that gives away her position.

VOSK
(shouting to the hunters, pointing)
THERE! THE ONE WITH THE GLOWING HAND! THAT ONE'S WORTH A FORTUNE!

The bounty hunters fall on Stella. She fights, but there are too many. They pin her. They tag her with a containment collar that kills her suit.

The last thing she sees before they haul her onto a slaver ship is Vosk — grinning, already counting his reward.

What Vosk hasn't figured out is that the hunters don't make distinctions. A net drops over him too.

VOSK (CONT'D)
(struggling)
No, no, wait, I handed her to you! I—!

They take him as well.

SEQ. 14 — INT. GAMBLING STATION — THE PIT — DAY21:46 → 23:015 blocks · #78-82

A market-station at the far edge of the galaxy. Slave auctions. And below, the heart of the business: THE PIT — a combat well ringed with stands, where they bet to the death.

An announcer presents the "Orb thief, the most wanted criminal in the sector." The stands roar. Stella, in the center, collar still on, looks for an exit that doesn't exist.

A gate opens on the opposite side of the well. They shove her opponent through.

He's a human. NOAH — young, battered, an old sword and a light weapon, eyes that don't give up.

Stella freezes. Because this boy looks like her. Like her people. (She'll understand it later: her planet used to banish the condemned to a far-off world to die. They didn't die. They became humanity. They are the children of her people's exiles.)

The crowd demands blood. Noah raises the sword, on guard. Stella doesn't.

STELLA
I'm not going to kill you so they can applaud.
NOAH
(sizing her up, not lowering the weapon)
…Is this a trick?
STELLA
It's an offer.
NOAH
(beat — he decides)
Then you're going to have to help me get out of here.

And instead of fighting each other, the two of them turn on the guards. Stella rips the collar off in one pull — the Orb flares back to life in her hand — and the pit turns into a jailbreak.

SEQ. 15 — INT. GAMBLING STATION — THE PIT / CATWALKS — CONTINUOUS23:01 → 24:205 blocks · #83-87

Chaos. Stella and Noah fight back to back. Brog and Nima come through the outer wall — Theron sent for her.

In the middle of the uproar, on an execution platform above the pit, the owners of the place are about to put down a prisoner who's no longer of use to them.

It's VOSK. On his knees, a weapon at the back of his head.

Stella sees him. She stops. Noah shouts at her to move.

And Stella — the one Vosk sold, betrayed, handed over — changes direction and goes for him.

She breaks the platform, deflects the shot, hauls him up. Vosk stares at her without understanding, his neck still marked by the weapon.

VOSK
(dazed)
…I gave you up. I sold you. Why would you—?
STELLA
Because I'm not you.

She shoves him loose toward the exit. And for the first time, the thug looks at her differently. Something in him breaks and reassembles.

They all escape together — Stella, Noah, Brog, Nima, Vosk — while the station burns behind them.

Later, aboard the ship, THERON watches Stella from a distance. He says nothing. But something has changed: the brat he picked up just saved the man who betrayed her. For a twenty-thousand-year-old hunter, that's worth more than the shine in her hand.

Noah, bandaging a wound, can't take his eyes off Stella. She doesn't even notice. She's looking at the sky, toward where Erdia is.

SEQ. 16 — EXT. COMET PLANET / INT. WORKSHOP — MONTAGE — [STELLA: ~YEAR 3]24:20 → 26:126 blocks · #88-93

Montage. The strange family that slowly healed her.

BROG and Stella, sitting in front of a sky streaked with comets. The giant teaches her his dead language — guttural, ancient words that no one else in the universe has left. Stella repeats them. Brog nods, slowly. It's the closest thing to a smile a mountain has.

NIMA, in the workshop, builds her a new suit — light, made to her measure, with a wrist monitor identical to the one that read "MAX" that day. Four hands working at once.

NIMA
(adjusting it)
Your old suit was a child's. This one belongs to whoever walks back into that palace.

And Stella training with the Orb. Unstable at first — discharges that throw her to the floor, that blow out panels. Then, little by little, channeling it: the light comes out of her palm, controlled, like a river that finally learns to let itself run.

For the first time since the escape, Stella doesn't look like a survivor. She looks like a weapon that chose its cause.

SEQ. 17 — INT. MOTHERSHIP — BRIDGE — NIGHT26:12 → 27:385 blocks · #94-98

Theron and Stella, alone. He asks her for something with a gesture.

THERON
Show me the hand. And the Orb.

Stella hesitates. Then opens her palm. The Orb beats beneath the skin, exposed.

Theron leans in. He studies it with a hunter's attention — and for an instant, his hand moves closer, like a man about to tear it out. Stella holds her breath.

But Theron stops. He reads something in how the Orb is tangled into her blood, into every vein. He withdraws his hand.

THERON
(to himself, almost)
No. Taking it out by force would kill you… and maybe whoever tried.
(he looks at her)
You're still more useful breathing.
STELLA
(putting the hand away)
Comforting.
(beat)
Theron. On my world they say things about the King. That he's immortal. That he never aged.
(beat)
That he arrived hundreds of years ago, out of nowhere.

Theron goes very still. Something lights up behind his eyes — that sounds like his prey. But he says nothing. It's still only a rumor.

THERON
(closing the subject)
Kings always say they're eternal. Get some sleep.

Stella goes. Theron stays looking out at the void, chewing on a twenty-thousand-year-old suspicion.

SEQ. 18 — INT. MOTHERSHIP — STELLA'S QUARTERS — NIGHT27:38 → 28:514 blocks · #99-102

Stella isn't sleeping. Theron comes in and sets something on the table: an image projector.

THERON
I don't care about your planet. But a good hunter always looks before he discards.
(beat)
You look too.

He switches it on. And there they are, alive, her people:

NAIO — old, in chains, a slave in a mist mine.

SELKA — no longer a baby, a young soldier training with surgical coldness, perfect, empty, her mother's same grey-blue eyes, and a scar over her left eye.

And a hooded figure leading the resistance alongside Marek. The hood falls back for a second. It's VERA.

Alive.

Stella brings a hand to her mouth. She isn't breathing.

STELLA
(a shattered whisper)
…She's alive. Mama's alive.

Theron watches her from the doorway. For the first time he isn't looking at bait. He's looking at someone who is going to do something impossible.

He leaves. Stella stays up all night in front of the image of her mother, staring into nothing.

SEQ. 19 — INT. ERDIA — MIST MINES — [ERDIA · YEAR 14] — DAY28:51 → 30:115 blocks · #103-107

TITLE CARD: ERDIA · YEAR 14.

The hooded figure — VERA — slips among the slaves of a mine. Searching. She finds him.

NAIO. An old man, broken, unrecognizable. Vera lowers her hood just enough for him to see her.

NAIO
(no air in him)
…Vera? No. No, you died. I saw you—

She's alive, Naio. Selka is alive.
(her voice cracks)
But she isn't… she isn't her anymore. They put something else inside her.

Don't go. If she's with him, it's a trap. I'm begging you.

(taking his face, decided)
I'll come back for you. I swear it. But first I'm going for my daughter.

SEQ. 20 — INT. IMPERIAL PALACE — THE GENERAL'S QUARTERS — [ERDIA · YEAR 14] — NIGHT30:11 → 33:3712 blocks · #108-119

Vera enters, hooded, with her loyalists KORIN and LESSA covering outside. MAREK walks her to the door.

MAREK
I'll keep watch here. If anything goes wrong, run. Don't look back.
(he grips her shoulder)
Bring her back.

Vera goes in alone.

Inside, with her back turned, stands SELKA — the General. Young, white, immaculate, cold, grey-blue eyes, a scar over her left eye.

SELKA
(without turning)
I know what you are. A resistance rat. Have you come to kill me in my sleep?
VERA
(lowering her hood)
I've come for you.

Selka turns. She sees Vera's face. And something, very deep down, trembles for a microsecond — before the conditioning crushes it.

SELKA
Traitor.

She attacks. They fight — but Vera doesn't strike back. She evades, blocks, gives ground. She is not capable of hurting her daughter.

VERA
(between blows)
I won't hurt you. I can't. Look at me. Look at me.

And Selka, faced with an enemy who refuses to wound her, comes apart. Her arm shakes. She lowers the weapon. Something in her surrenders, without understanding why.

Vera folds her into an embrace. Holds her the way mothers hold.

VERA (CONT'D)
(in her ear, weeping)
Let's go home. You, me, and your father. Let's start over. Let's forget all of this.

Selka, inside the embrace, closes her eyes.

And drives a weapon into her side.

Vera chokes back a cry. Her hand rises to Selka's face — that face — searching for her, not understanding.

VERA
(a thread of a voice, broken, betrayed)
…Selka. Why.

Selka holds her against herself — the embrace becomes something else — and whispers something in her ear that we do NOT get to hear.

From the half-open door, KORIN and LESSA see all of it: the General holding her mother, and the knife. Marek arrives one second late, his face torn open with horror.

KORIN
(whispering, backing away)
…She killed her. Her own daughter killed her.

Vera slides to the floor. She looks at Selka's face — that face — until the very last second. And she dies believing it.

Selka stands over her mother's body, the weapon dripping, not understanding why she's shaking all over, or why, suddenly, without meaning to, she is humming a lullaby she doesn't remember ever learning.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF ACT TWO.

ACT THREE · 転結 (TEN · KETSU) — THE RETURN AND THE THRONE

SEQ. 21 — EXT. ERDIA — RUINED PORT — [ERDIA · YEAR 20] — DAY33:37 → 35:417 blocks · #120-126

TITLE CARD: ERDIA · YEAR 20.

What was once a golden city is grey now, and split in two. Fog. Soldiers. Murals of the King everywhere, and beneath them, scrawled in fury, one name crossed out: STELLA.

A small ship lands among the scrap. Out come BROG, NIMA, NOAH — and a young woman with her hair dyed dark, hooded. STELLA, ~19. In disguise on her own world.

She moves through the crowd. And the people, not recognizing her, look at her with the hatred the King taught them. An old man spits when he hears her name in somebody else's conversation.

NOAH
(low, beside her)
A whole planet hates you. And you came back anyway.
STELLA
(looking at the ruins)
That's why I came back.

A resistance patrol intercepts them, weapons up. At the front, older, wearier: KORIN.

He aims at Stella. She lowers her hood slowly. Korin looks at her face — he doesn't recognize her. The hair isn't hers. He's about to give the order to fire.

And then, beneath the dyed hair, he recognizes her face — the girl he knew twenty years ago, always at her mother's side.

KORIN
(his voice breaking)
…Stella? I knew you when you were small — always beside your mother.
(he lowers the weapon, shaking)
It's you. You came back.

IN ORBIT — INT. THERON'S MOTHERSHIP: VOSK watches the screen, where Erdia is boiling. He clenches his fists. He turns to Theron.

VOSK
Let me go down. She's alone down there.
THERON
It isn't our war.
VOSK
(beat)
It became mine the day she pulled me out of that pit.

Theron looks at him. He doesn't say yes. He doesn't stop him either.

SEQ. 22 — INT. ERDIA — RESISTANCE SHELTER — DAY35:41 → 37:065 blocks · #127-131

Korin brings Stella before an old slave the resistance has just freed. Stooped, blind in one eye, unrecognizable.

NAIO.

The air goes out of Stella. She comes closer. She kneels in front of him.

STELLA
(barely)
Papa.

Naio lifts his head. He searches for her face and can't find it — until he takes her hand. And feels, beneath the skin, the light-key. The Orb. Beating.

His good eye fills with tears.

NAIO
You came back. Twenty years. I told you never to let him have it, and… look at you. You kept it safe. Your whole life.
(he touches her dyed hair)
My river girl.
STELLA
Mama's alive, Papa. I saw her. I'm getting you both out of here. Selka too. I promise you.

Naio squeezes her hand. He hasn't the heart to tell her what the resistance believes happened to Vera. He lets her go with her hope intact.

SEQ. 23 — EXT./INT. IMPERIAL PALACE — THE ASSAULT — DAY37:06 → 37:473 blocks · #132-134

The resistance attacks. War breaks out through the fog-choked streets. Brog punches breaches open; Nima disables robots; Noah covers Stella.

In the chaos, a strike team rescues a group of key slaves — Naio among them, carried to safety.

Stella was given a reconnaissance mission. She breaks it. She climbs. She scales the palace through the gunfire, alone, toward the highest dome — the Throne Room.

She reaches the glass roof. Below, at the center of the hall, a white figure stands waiting, motionless, as though it knew she was coming.

Stella shatters the glass with a blow from the Orb. And falls into the throne room.

SEQ. 24 — INT. IMPERIAL PALACE — THRONE ROOM — CONTINUOUS37:47 → 39:386 blocks · #135-140

Stella lands among the shards. She stands.

In front of her, SELKA — the General, 21, white, perfect, grey-blue eyes, scar over the left eye, not a flicker of expression.

The two sisters, face to face, for the first time in twenty years. Neither of them entirely knows it.

SELKA
The thief. Back at the scene of the crime.
(she draws a blade of light)
How considerate.
STELLA
(hands open, not attacking)
I didn't come here to fight you.
SELKA
Everyone says that before they die.

Selka attacks. And here we learn something: Stella could win. She trained with hunters from another universe; she's faster, stronger. She blocks, deflects, disarms her once. But she doesn't finish it. She doesn't want to beat her. She wants to reach her.

STELLA
(evading)
Why did you kill our mother?
SELKA
(without a blink)
I've executed hundreds. I don't remember any of them being called "mother."

That hurts Stella more than any blow. For a second her guard drops. Selka puts her on the floor, blade raised.

And Stella, cornered, desperate, without thinking — on pure older-sister reflex — makes the gesture.

She cups her palm. A spark of Gix dances in her hand. And she raises it toward Selka's face, reaching for her forehead.

The same gesture from the laboratory. The game with the light.

Selka freezes. The blade trembles in the air. Her body recognizes that gesture — that safety — before her mind understands where from. Something buried for twenty years stirs. Beneath the General, for an instant, a little girl surfaces.

SELKA
(a whisper, lost)
…Why… why do I know that?

The two of them still. One heartbeat from waking.

And it is that heartbeat that terrifies Selka. She has nowhere to put what she's feeling. So she does the only thing she was ever taught: she attacks what confuses her.

She grabs Stella's hand — and tears the Orb out. She can: they share the same blood.

The Orb comes out of Stella's palm in a flash of light. Stella collapses. She doesn't die — she falls into a coma, eyes rolled back.

A spark leaves the wristband and slips into Stella's chest: Gix, wrapping himself around her heart from the inside so it won't stop.

Selka stands there, the Orb in her hand, breathing hard, not knowing what she has just done to her own body.

SELKA (CONT'D)
(to the guards, her voice not quite steady)
Spread the word. The traitor has fallen.

SEQ. 25 — INT. THRONE ROOM — CONTINUOUS39:38 → 40:283 blocks · #141-143

Selka walks to the throne's console. She sets the Orb into its cradle. At last. She is going to give Erdia back its light. She is going to be the hero she believes she is.

The light does not come back.

Instead, everything shakes. The palace walls fold in. The floor becomes a deck. The throne opens out like a command bridge. Ancient engines wake in the depths.

What we believed all film long was a palace — what the people believed for centuries was a meteorite buried in their city — powers up, all of it.

It was always a ship.

Selka backs away, afraid for the first time in her life.

SELKA
(to the shadow on the throne)
Majesty… the Orb should bring the light back. What is this? What's happening?

VORTHAN (O.S.)
The light was never for them, little one.

And it was never yours.

SEQ. 26 — INT. THRONE ROOM — CONTINUOUS — "IT WAS ME"40:28 → 43:2911 blocks · #144-154

SEQ. 27 — INT. PALACE-SHIP — THRONE DECK — CONTINUOUS43:29 → 44:394 blocks · #155-158

The thing drops every disguise. And takes its true form.
